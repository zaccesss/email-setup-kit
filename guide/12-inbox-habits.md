# Inbox habits and teams

A tidy setup still needs a few habits to stay tidy.

## Daily habits

- **Archive, do not delete.** Archived mail stays searchable. Delete only spam and true junk.
- **Let filters do the sorting.** Anything that arrives regularly and needs no reply should skip the
  inbox and get a label.
- **Snooze** mail you cannot deal with yet, instead of leaving it unread.
- **Flag or star** only what needs action, then clear the flag when it is done.
- **Unsubscribe** from anything you have not opened in a month.

## Out-of-office replies

- Turn them on only while you are away, with an end date.
- Reply only to people in your contacts where the option exists. Replying to every sender confirms
  your address to spammers.
- Say when you are back and who to contact if it is urgent. Keep it short.

```text
Hello,

Thank you for your email. I am away until [date] with limited access to email and will reply when
I am back.

If your message is urgent, please contact [name] at [address].

Kind regards,
[Your name]
```

## Phones

- Use the provider's own app where possible. Filters and labels work the same because they run on
  the server.
- Phone apps often have their own signature setting. Use a short plain-text one there.

## Teams and shared addresses

| Need | Option |
| --- | --- |
| Several people answer one address (`support@`) | A group or shared mailbox, so everyone sees replies |
| One person covers several addresses | Aliases on that person's mailbox |
| Someone leaves | Convert their mailbox to shared or forward it, rather than deleting it straight away |

> [!NOTE]
> In Google Workspace, groups can be created on the primary domain or a secondary domain, but not
> on a user alias domain. Plan shared addresses with that in mind.
